Our Identity & Heritage

About Hiranyakeshi Agrotech

Building an integrated agro-value chain that empowers farmers and delivers unmatched grain quality to consumers.

Who We Are

A Transformative Force in Grain Agriculture

Hiranyakeshi Agrotech Pvt. Ltd. is totally women-led agri-business enterprise built with a clear purpose—to create a stronger connection between farmers, sustainable agriculture, modern processing and the market.

We grow paddy through our own farms and also source it directly from farmers, ensuring quality and value at every step of the journey. Currently, our primary grain handled is paddy (rice) only, allowing us to bring laser-sharp focus and operational depth to every single kernel.

Our Story

Hiranyakeshi Agrotech was founded with a simple vision — to bridge the gap between farmers and consumers by adding real value at every stage, from procurement to distribution. By taking ownership of the value chain, we ensure farmers receive honest returns while consumers receive rice of uncompromised purity and standard.

Bridging the Gap Direct Farm to Table
Core Capabilities

Our Integrated Approach

End-to-end synergy across cultivation, processing, packaging, and pan-India distribution.

Own Farms

Growing quality paddy directly on company-managed acreage using scientifically optimized cultivation and harvest techniques.

Procurement

Sourcing directly from trusted farmer networks with calibrated digital weighbridges and fair spot-market valuations.

Quality Control

Ensuring every batch meets high benchmarks for moisture stability, grain length, chalkiness, and purity.

Processing, Grading & Sorting

State-of-the-art milling, color sortex optical grading, and destoning ensuring uniform, export-ready grain appearance.

Packaging & Branding

Consumer-ready moisture-proof packaging branded with traceability and verified nutritional standards.

Marketing & Distribution

Farm to consumer, seamlessly — fulfilling bulk grain demands for mills, food companies, and retail distributor channels.

Value Proposition

Why Hiranyakeshi Agrotech

Strategic differentiators that redefine modern agri-commodity supply chains.

Farm-to-Consumer Model

Farmer Welfare at Core
