About Company

From Soil to Soul - A legacy Rooted in Trust

We connect with every paddy-growing farmer in our region — many of whom depend on this crop for their livelihood — and build a fair, organized, and scientifically-driven procurement network that secures our supply chain while improving farmer income and long-term economic resilience.

Hiranyakeshi Agrotech Pvt. Ltd. is an agri-business enterprise focused on creating an integrated and sustainable agricultural value chain by connecting farmers with scientific cultivation, organized procurement, modern food processing, value addition, branding and markets. The company aims to improve farmer realization by moving beyond the conventional sale of raw agricultural produce and developing higher-value processed and branded products

End-to-End Excellence

Integrated Value Chain Overview

A seamless 9-step quality lifecycle ensuring uncompromised purity and higher value from seed to table.

1

Procurement

Sourcing pristine paddy directly from our own farms & trusted farmer partners at transparent rates.

2

Quality Control

Rigorous multi-parameter moisture, purity, and grain integrity testing before intake.

3

Processing

Modern milling and de-husking with gentle temperature and moisture management.

4

Grading

Precision dimension, density, and grain-length grading for consistent culinary output.

5

Sorting

High-speed optical Sortex machines removing discolored or imperfect kernels.

6

Packaging

Food-grade, tamper-proof, and humidity-sealed eco bags preserving farm freshness.

7

Branding

Trust-backed product positioning assuring authentic origin and certified nutrition.

8

Marketing

Connecting bulk institutional buyers, retailers, and households with transparent pricing.

9

Distribution

Reliable nationwide logistics delivering farm-fresh paddy and rice safely on schedule.
