What We Export/Supply

Paddy (Raw)

Carefully harvested, sun-dried & mechanically cleaned paddy grains direct from origin farms.

  • Varieties: Basmati, Non-Basmati, Sona Masoori, Wada Kolam
  • Moisture Content: ≤ 13% – 14% Calibrated
  • Foreign Matter / Chaff: < 1% Pre-cleaned
  • Dispatch Formats: Loose Bulk / Jute Bags / 50kg PP
