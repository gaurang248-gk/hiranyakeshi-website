Direct Farm Benefits

Why Partner with Hiranyakeshi Agrotech?

Traditional grain supply chains often deprive farmers of their rightful earnings through middleman deductions, opaque weighing, and delayed payments. We change that completely.

✓

Direct Farm-Gate Procurement

No need to transport your produce to far-off mandis or pay loading commission fees.

✓

Calibrated Digital Weighing & Instant Quality Assay

Accurate digital scales and transparent moisture checks carried out in your presence.

✓

Prompt & Secure Electronic Bank Payments

Direct transfers to your bank account without delays, intermediaries, or credit risks.

✓

Soil & Crop Advisory Guidance

Access to best practices on seed selection, bio-fertilizers, and sustainable water management to maximize acreage yield.

Seamless Partnership

How Farmers Join Our Network

A simple 4-step onboarding journey built on mutual trust and transparency.

1

Register Your Details

Fill in our simple partner form or call our field coordinator with your farm location and estimated paddy acreage.

2

Harvest Assessment

Our agricultural field officer visits your farm to assess crop variety, maturity, and agree on harvest pickup dates.

3

Weighing & Quality Testing

Certified digital weighment and rapid moisture inspection conducted with complete transparency in front of you.

4

Direct Bank Settlement

Transparent invoice generated and funds credited straight to your registered bank account promptly.
