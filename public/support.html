Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick solutions to common questions from our partners and farmers.

How do I register as a farmer partner?

Visit our For Farmers page to review our procurement guidelines and submit your registration details. Our field team connects with registered farmers within 2–3 working days.

What paddy and rice varieties do you handle?

We specialize in GI-tagged Ajara Ghansal, Indrayani, Kala Jirga, Jondhala Jirga, and other premium regional varieties. View our complete range on our Products page.

How are procurement rates and payments determined?

Rates are established transparently based on grain quality, moisture content, and current fair market valuations with on-time direct settlements.

How do I place bulk B2B or export orders?

Please submit your specifications through our Export & B2B page, or write directly to info@hiranyakeshiagrotech.com.
